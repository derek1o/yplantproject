What You'll Learn

Photography

Learn to shoot with professional cameras, composition, lighting, and editing your best shots.

Videography

Plan, shoot, and direct short films and social videos from start to finish.

Drones

Fly safely, capture aerial footage, and understand drone regulations.

Editing & Post-Production

Cut, colour, and polish footage using professional editing software.

Social Media Content

Create reels, posts, and campaigns that actually get seen.

Coding & Web Development

Build your own websites and apps with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript - beginner-friendly.

About the Programme

Y Plant Affricanaidd CIC is excited to launch a brand-new digital empowerment programme designed specifically for young people aged 12 to 18. Whether you want to be behind the camera, directing, editing, flying drones, or building websites, this programme is your gateway into the digital world.

Proudly funded by BBC Children in Need.
